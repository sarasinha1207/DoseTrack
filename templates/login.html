DoseGuard AI

DoseGuard AI is a clinical family medication safety and adherence platform engineered to protect elderly parents and grandparents managing chronic, multi-drug therapies. Built to eliminate missed doses and dangerous accidental double-dosing, the platform combines individual member schedules, cross-family care transparency, local health vaults, and cognitive math alertness verification to safeguard our loved ones' health while preserving their independence.

Dedicated to supporting family caregivers with reliable, local-first oversight and dignity for aging family members.
