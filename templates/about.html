Clinical Rationale & Purpose

Protecting Elder Independence Through Accountable Family Care

DoseTrack was conceived not as an abstract productivity tracker, but as a practical clinical safety net for elderly parents and grandparents managing multiple prescription therapies each day.

The Geriatric Polypharmacy Reality

Why standard smartphone alarms and generic reminder apps fail our elderly family members.

Polypharmacy Overload

Seniors over age 65 typically take between 4 and 9 distinct daily prescription medications across staggered schedules (fasting morning, post-lunch, bedtime). Complex pill regimens create confusion and anxiety.

The "Dismiss & Forget" Habit

Standard smartphone alarms allow single-tap silencing. An elderly parent will dismiss an alert while walking to the kitchen, get sidetracked by a household chore, and leave critical medication unconsumed.

Double-Dosing Hazards

When seniors cannot remember if they already took their blood pressure tablet or insulin dose, the fear of missing a dose leads to dangerous accidental duplicate consumption.

Clinical Fact
According to the World Health Organization and geriatric safety studies, non-adherence to long-term therapies results in severe health complications, avoidable hospitalizations, and significant caregiver stress. Clear visibility combined with cognitive verification drastically improves therapy compliance.

The Cognitive Alertness Challenge

How solving two simple arithmetic puzzles bridges the gap between alarm perception and physical pill intake.

Prefrontal Cortex Activation

Solving basic arithmetic (addition and multiplication) shifts the brain out of passive auto-pilot. By requiring conscious engagement, the user becomes fully alert to their surrounding environment and physical task.

Intentional Medication Confirmation

Because the alarm continues chiming until the math challenges are verified, the user is directly focused on the screen, which displays the exact medication name, dosage, and food instructions.

Calibrated Cognitive Load

Puzzles are designed to be accessible yet intentional (such as 28 + 19 or 7 x 5). They do not cause frustration, but are sufficiently engaging to prevent accidental dismissal during grogginess.

Cross-Generational Care Coordination

DoseTrack unifies the household while respecting the personal autonomy of each relative.

The Primary Admin Caregiver

Families designate an anchor caregiver (such as an adult daughter or son). The Primary Admin can update biometrics, enter new prescriptions, verify schedules, and inspect adherence records across all connected relatives.

Shared Transparency Tab

The "All Family Medications" view lists every member alongside their active therapies. Anyone in the household can glance at the board and confirm whether grandparents took their mid-afternoon supplements.

Individual Personal Dashboards

Each family member signs in with their dedicated username and password. Their personal view highlights their own schedule, adherence percentage, and recorded biometric profile.

Reliable, Lightweight Tech Stack

Engineered for swift deployment on Render with minimal resource overhead and maximum reliability.

Python FastAPI Backend

Fast, asynchronous RESTful endpoints with Pydantic type validation, session management, and deterministic prescription analysis algorithms.

Semantic Vanilla Web Layer

Clean HTML5, modular CSS3 custom properties, and modern Vanilla JavaScript without heavy frontend framework bloat or hydration penalties.

Web Audio API Synthesis

Synthesized audio tones generated natively in the browser without external sound file downloads or network latency delays.
