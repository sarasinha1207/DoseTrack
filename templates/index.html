Comprehensive Elder Medication Safety

Designed specifically for adult children, elderly parents, and grandparents managing complex chronic treatment schedules.

Personal Member Dashboards

Each family member possesses their own secure profile recording biometrics (weight, height, blood group, age), primary care physician, and specific scheduled intake windows.

Cross-Family Schedule Transparency

Any family member can view the active medications and compliance logs of parents and grandparents. Caregivers instantly see if morning doses were verified or missed.

Dual-Puzzle Math Wakeup Alarm

Audio alerts cannot be reflexively silenced or snoozed. Seniors must solve two simple arithmetic math problems to confirm cognitive alertness before the alarm turns off.

Clinical Prescription Intake

Integrated support for hospital discharge summaries, prescription slips, and pharmacy bills. The AI engine extracts drug names, dosages, intervals, and food instructions.

Primary Family Admin Control

Designate a primary family caregiver (such as an adult daughter or son) who holds elevated management privileges to add regimens, update dosages, and supervise all members.

Zero-Cloud Vault Security

Elder health records, biometric indicators, and medication schedules stay strictly confidential within your local vault, eliminating third-party commercial data harvesting.

How DoseGuard AI Operates

A four-step clinical workflow designed for straightforward everyday adoption by seniors and adult children.

Step 1

Register Member Profile

Sign up with basic credentials, followed by clinical profile completion including weight, height, blood group, and physician details.

Step 2

Enter Medication Regimen

Add medication names, prescribed dosages, target times (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night), and specific food intake guidance.

Step 3

Auditory Alarm & Math Verification

At intake time, the Web Audio alarm activates. The senior solves 2 arithmetic problems to confirm alertness before logging the dose.

Step 4

Family Caregiver Confirmation

All connected family members view real-time adherence indicators across all relatives from the Family Overview tab.
